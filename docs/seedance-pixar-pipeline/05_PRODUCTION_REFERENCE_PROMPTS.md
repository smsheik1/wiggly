A character model sheet from a feature-quality 3D animated film, production CG, not photorealistic and not flat cartoon, laid out on a single seamless flat neutral-grey studio background, exact same flat mid-grey colour #B5B5B5 across the full canvas, with no gradient, no vignette and no shadow on the backdrop, soft even studio lighting throughout.

Reading left to right, top row then bottom row, the eight expressions are: 1) NEUTRAL — face relaxed, mouth closed, eyes level. 2) HAPPY — a warm closed-lip smile, eyes softly crinkled. 3) DELIGHTED — eyes wide and bright, mouth open in a huge unguarded grin showing the gap where one upper front tooth is missing. 4) SAD — inner brows lifted, mouth turned down at the corners, eyes wet and lowered. 5) SURPRISED — eyes very wide, brows high, mouth open in a small round shape. 6) CONFUSED — one brow up and one down, mouth small and pushed to one side, eyes searching off to the side. 7) ANGRY — brows low and drawn together, mouth pressed into a firm line, chin tucked. 8) TALKING — caught mid-word, mouth open in speech, eyes engaged and alive.

## 3. Location Plate: The Kindergarten Classroom

```text

Camera: wide shot at adult chest height, straight on, looking slightly down at the floor area. The whole room reads to the frame edges.

Room: a small kindergarten classroom on a sunny morning, completely empty of people. A large soft rug in the centre foreground patterned with bright teal, sunflower-yellow and grass-green shapes, with twelve square carpet mats in mixed bright colours laid out in loose uneven rows, slightly scuffed and not perfectly aligned. Behind it, a low bookshelf of open cubbies painted in alternating yellow, teal and orange, stacked with picture books lying at angles. A warm white wall above it covered edge to edge with children's crayon drawings taped up unevenly at different heights, the paper curling at the corners. A hand-lettered paper banner reading MY FAMILY is strung across the wall above the drawings in thick uneven marker capitals, drooping slightly in the middle.

Secondary detail receding into depth: low tables with small stacking chairs in bright blue and green on the left, a coat rack with colourful empty pegs and one forgotten cardigan, a small aquarium tank on a low stand on the right with green plants and clear water, soft morning sunlight casting long gentle shadows across the floor from unseen tall windows on the left.

## 4. Multi-Shot Video Prompt: Scene 2 (Extend / Sequel)
* **Target Engine**: Seedance 2.5 (1080p, 29s)
* **Elements Attached**: `@classroom`, `@location1`, `@Mia`, `@Ben`, `@Sofia`, `@Miss_Park`, `@ben-drawing`, `@mia-drawing1`, `@sofia-drawing`

```text
[REFERENCE MATERIALS]  
@classroom = the kindergarten classroom. Use only the space, materials, colours and morning light.  
@location1 = the seating arrangement and the geometry of the room. The round rug, the ring of carpet squares, where each child sits, where the presenting child and the teacher stand, and where the camera sits relative to all of it. Do not use the faces of the children in this reference.  
@Mia = the smallest five-year-old girl, two bunches at uneven heights, pink scrunchie high and lilac low.  
@Ben = the five-year-old boy with large protruding ears and a black buzz cut, navy and cream striped tee.  
@Sofia = the tallest five-year-old girl, huge round cloud of curly mid-brown hair, grey long-sleeve top and magenta tutu.  
@Miss_Park = the one adult, young woman, mustard cardigan, round tortoiseshell glasses, black low ponytail.  
@ben-drawing = Ben's crayon drawing.  
@mia-drawing1 = Mia's crayon drawing.  
@sofia-drawing = Sofia's crayon drawing.

Each reference is one person or one object and appears once. No duplicates.

[VOICES]  
@Ben VOICE: "A 5-year-old American boy. Bright, light and a little husky; fast and eager, tumbling over his own words when he gets going, breath running out at the end of a sentence; happy-go-lucky and hugely, unashamedly proud of his father, and completely sincere on the last line."  
@Mia VOICE: "A 5-year-old American girl. Bright, high and slightly breathy; fast and tumbling, words running together and rising at the end when she gets excited; completely sincere, with no self-consciousness at all."  
@Sofia VOICE: "A 5-year-old American girl. Very quiet, thin and breathy, low in volume throughout; hesitant and halting, starting a word and restarting it, with small pauses in the wrong places; shy and awkward, warm underneath it, and not unhappy."  
@Miss_Park VOICE: "A 25-year-old American woman. Bright, light and a little thin at the top of her range; quick and eager, sentences tumbling out slightly faster than she means them to, with small self-conscious laughs and the odd stumble she corrects cheerfully; hugely warm and enthusiastic, and visibly delighted by these children."  
The class VOICE: "A room of American five-year-olds, all different voices at different pitches. Ragged and overlapping, never landing together, each one saying something different at once."

[GOAL]  
A nine-shot, twenty-seven-second segment inside a two-minute animated comedy. Ben finishes his turn at the front of a classroom and returns to his seat, the teacher asks who is next, one child throws her hand up and is passed over, and a shy child is called instead and begins her turn. This is one of several linked segments. It ends the instant the shy child's first line finishes — do not continue into her story, do not resolve anything, do not add an ending.  
The comedy is played completely straight. Nobody in frame knows anything is funny. The room is an entirely ordinary classroom.

[CONTINUITY]  
Location: the room in @classroom, morning, throughout. Never leave it. The ring of children and the camera side match @location1 in every shot.  
Ten children, every carpet square in the ring occupied, each holding their own crayon drawing on their knees. @Sofia and @Mia sit next to each other on the right-hand arc of the ring, @Sofia first and @Mia further to the right in frame. @Ben's square is on the left-hand arc and is empty until he returns to it in Shot 3.  
The other seven children are seven completely different ordinary five-year-olds — a deliberate mix of skin tones, hair colours, hair lengths and builds, each clearly distinct from the other six. None resembles @Mia, @Ben or @Sofia, and no face in the room repeats.  
@Miss_Park is the only adult and stands at the far side of the ring beside the presenting child. She stays there throughout Shots 1 to 8.  
Costume is fixed for the whole segment and never changes.

[SHOTS]

SHOT 1 | 0–3s  
Wide, exactly the framing of @location1 — the whole ring of seated children, the presenter and the teacher at the far side facing camera. @Ben stands there holding @ben-drawing up, @Miss_Park beside him. The class applauds, the reaction travelling raggedly around the ring rather than starting together, some children late to it, a few twisting to look at each other. On the right-hand arc one small girl, @Mia, does not clap — head right down over @mia-drawing1 on her knees, shoulders working. Camera holds, no move.  
A child in the class: {Yeah!}  
Another child in the class: {That was so good!}  
Hard cut.

SHOT 2 | 3–5s  
Close on @Mia sitting cross-legged on the right-hand arc, low and level with her, the ring falling away behind her. Head down, tongue between her teeth, working hard at @mia-drawing1 on her knees, oblivious to the noise. The drawing is angled away from camera and no part of it is readable. Camera holds.  
cut to Extreme close-up, right down on the paper — the tip of a red crayon in a small fist scrubbing back and forth over @mia-drawing1, hard and fast, the fibres of the paper lifting. The frame is filled entirely by the moving crayon and a patch of dense red, with no figures, no edges of the sheet and nothing else of the drawing in shot. Camera holds.  
Hard cut.

SHOT 3 | 5–9s  
Medium two-shot at the far side of the ring, past the heads of two seated children in the foreground. @Miss_Park stands beside @Ben, turned slightly down toward him, and puts one hand flat on his shoulder — on the shoulder. @Ben tips his head back to look up at her, holding @ben-drawing against his chest in both hands. @Ben turns and walks out of frame, and says thank you as he walks away. Camera holds.  
@Miss_Park: {Well done, Ben! That was great.}  
@Ben: {Thank you, Miss Park.}  
Hard cut.

SHOT 4 | 9–11s  
Medium on @Miss_Park at the far side of the ring, facing camera, the seated children's heads in the foreground. Her hands come together in front of her. She lifts her chin and addresses the whole room. Camera holds.  
@Miss_Park: {Okay — so who's next?}  
Hard cut.

SHOT 5 | 11–14s  
Close on @Mia, same framing as Shot 2. Her head snaps up on the word next. She looks straight back down, puts in one more fast scribble anyway, drops the crayon, flips @mia-drawing1 face-down and clamps it against her chest with one arm, and shoots her other hand straight up above her head. Camera holds.  
@Mia: {Me! Me Miss Park! I want to go next!}  
Hard cut.

SHOT 6 | 14–18s  
Medium on @Miss_Park at the far side of the ring, facing camera, warm and amused, her eyes fixed on @Mia off frame.  
@Miss_Park: {That's wonderful enthusiasm, Mia, but let's give others a turn.}  
@Miss_Park: {Sofia? Would you like to share?}  
Hard cut.

SHOT 7 | 18–23s  
Close on @Sofia sitting cross-legged on the right-hand arc, head down and looking at her lap, @sofia-drawing on her knees turned face down, drawing not visible. The camera pans smoothly right along the ring and arrives on @Mia sitting beside her, still holding the drawing against her chest, her other hand straight up and her whole body bobbing up and down on the spot, still seated, other arm waving. The camera holds on @Mia a moment, then pans smoothly back left to @Sofia, finding her with her head now lifted. She speaks. One continuous move — the camera never stops and never cuts.  
@Sofia: {Umm… okay, Miss Park.}  
Hard cut.

SHOT 8 | 23–26s  
Wide, exactly the framing of @location1 again. @Sofia gets up from her square on the right-hand arc and walks around the edge of the rug to the far side in small close steps, @sofia-drawing held low against her legs. She reaches to the end beside @Miss_Park, turns to face camera, and stands still. Camera holds.  
Hard cut.

SHOT 9 | 26–30s  
The same framing and the same move as the opening shot of Ben's presentation, with a different child. Opens tight on @Sofia turning around her paper, revealing @sofia-drawing filling the frame, held in both hands at chest height. The camera lifts off the paper and rises to find @Sofia's face, settling in close on her eyes as she looks out at the class. Slow continuous rise, the camera never stops and never cuts.  
@Sofia: {This is my family. My dad's retired, but Mom still works as a nurse.}  
Hard cut, end of segment.

[VISUAL STYLE]  
Production CG from a feature-quality 3D animated film. Stylised proportions, matte skin with warm subsurface glow through ears and fingertips, matte cloth with weight and few large folds. Not photorealistic, not flat cartoon, no film stock, no grain, no photographic skin texture.  
Morning daylight through the classroom windows, warm and soft, filling the room evenly. Cool bounce off the pale walls. No practicals, no atmosphere, no haze.  
Palette is muted and warm — pale wood, off-white walls, the coloured rug and carpet squares. The only saturated colours are the children's clothes and the crayon on their drawings. Keep deep red out of the room itself.  
Shallow depth of field on the close shots only, rendered as an in-camera artefact, not as film stock.

[CAMERA AND PERFORMANCE]  
Exactly nine shots and eight transitions. Every transition is a hard cut. No cut inside any shot. Two shots contain a continuous camera move — Shot 7's pan right, hold and pan back, and Shot 9's rise — and each is one unbroken move that must not be chopped into pieces.

@Ben: his objective is to be thanked properly and get back to his seat without making a fuss. He is solemn about it. Business is holding his drawing carefully in both hands. One beat change: he tips his head back to look up at the teacher, then turns away and goes.

@Mia: her objective is to be picked. Her whole body is aimed at the far side of the ring. Four beat changes — head down and working, oblivious to everything around her; the snap upward on the word next; the arm going up and staying up; and the bobbing, which is her sitting still failing. Business is the crayon and the paper, and the strongest accent in the segment is the moment she stops that business dead. She never sulks and never doubts herself. When she is passed over she does not lower the hand.

@Sofia: her objective is to not be looked at, and then to do as she is asked anyway. Two beat changes — head down at her lap; the slow lift of her head. She moves in small steps and holds her drawing turned in against herself. Her shoulders stay slightly rounded even at the front.

@Miss_Park: her objective is to keep the room moving and to get the quietest child to speak. Three beat changes — clapping with the class; turning out to the whole room; softening as her attention lands on the shy child. She is warm and never dismissive when she passes over the raised hand.

The other seven children: they react as individuals, staggered, never together, each doing something different — one still clapping late, one twisting round, one looking at their own paper. Never a unison movement and never a unison sound.

Motion: everything winds the opposite way before it goes. Mia's arm drops slightly before it shoots up. Heads and hands travel in curves, not straight lines. Fast between poses with a small overshoot on arrival that settles back. Hair and cloth arrive a beat after the body and keep going a beat past the stop — Sofia's hair mass especially, and Mia's bunches on every bob. Every held pose is a moving hold, with breathing, small weight shifts and blinks. Ben's walk has real ground contact and weight transfer. No even, sliding, game-engine motion anywhere.

Eye life is mandatory in every shot where a face is visible. Micro-saccades, live catchlights, realistic blink rate, and eyes reaching a target before the head turns to it.

The body plays broad. The face stays honest. No mugging, no pulled faces, no comic reactions.

[AUDIO]  
Ordinary classroom room tone throughout — a room with a hard floor and small bodies in it. Ragged applause and overlapping children's voices at the top, dying away unevenly rather than stopping together. Crayon scratching on paper. Paper crinkling. Soft footsteps on carpet. A single distant sound from outside the window.  
No music anywhere in this segment.

[EXCLUSIONS]  
The only spoken words in the entire segment are the lines in braces. Nothing else written anywhere in this prompt is dialogue, and nobody speaks who is not named on a line.  
No extra cuts anywhere — nine shots total, no cut inside any shot, and every transition a hard cut with no fades or dissolves.  
The camera stays on the same side of the ring as in @location1 for the whole segment and never crosses to the other side.  
@mia-drawing1 is never readable and never held up.  
Shot 7 is one continuous camera move that pans right and then pans back, not two separate shots.

[MAINTAIN CONSISTENCY]  
Ten children and one adult throughout, every square in the ring occupied, matching the layout in @location1. Every child's face is different from every other child's face and none repeats.  
@Mia, @Sofia, @Ben and @Miss_Park are one person each, appear once, and their references are never applied to any other child. @Mia is the smallest child and @Sofia the tallest in every shot they share.  
@Sofia and @Mia are on the right-hand arc with @Mia further right, and this never flips.  
Three distinct drawings — @ben-drawing, @mia-drawing1, @sofia-drawing — which never swap, and no fourth drawing appears.
```
