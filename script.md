# Steve Jobs: A Pixar Story — Narration Script

> Target duration per clip: 15 seconds.
> To allow for natural theatrical pauses, breathing, and visual holds, aim for **14–17 words** per chapter.

---

### Chapter 1: The Hidden Side of the Fence (Age 8)
**Visual:** Young Steve and father Paul at the workbench sanding the cedar fence board in the sunlit garage.  
**Current Script:**
```text
Long before any machine, Lisa, my father taught me in our garage that the back of the fence must be beautiful, even if nobody ever looks.
```

---

### Chapter 2: Dylan on the Turntable (Age 16)
**Visual:** Teen Steve and Woz pushing the red-and-white VW camper van at sunset.  
**Current Script:**
```text
We had an old Volkswagen bus and zero money. Late at night, listening to Dylan, I realized poetry and circuits weren't opposites. They were love letters.
```

---

### Chapter 3: The Bicycle for the Mind (Age 22)
**Visual:** Young Steve and Woz in the dark garage as the wooden computer keyboard triggers the glowing green CRT screen.  
**Current Script:**
```text
I slept on cold floors and returned soda bottles to eat. When that first letter glowed on an old TV screen, our hearts nearly burst.
```

---

### Chapter 4: The Only Masterpiece (Age 50)
**Visual:** Mature Steve in black turtleneck and spectacles holding the framed portrait of Lisa at golden sunset.  
**Current Script:**
```text
I was twenty-three and terrified, Lisa. I spent years trying to build perfect machines, but you were always the only masterpiece that truly mattered.
```
