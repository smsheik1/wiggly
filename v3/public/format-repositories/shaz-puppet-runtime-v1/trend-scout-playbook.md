# Shaz Trend Scout Playbook (Cultural Radar)

This playbook defines how the Shaz format repository tracks breaking cultural trends, leaks, and news early by monitoring the **Layer 1 Forensic Sieve** on Reddit.

---

## 1. Why Layer 1 (Reddit) Over Layer 2 (X)

- **The Problem with Raw X:** Raw insider tweets have zero community vetting, full edge-blocking (Cloudflare), and reply sections filled with bots and blue-check spam.
- **The Reddit Advantage:** Enthusiast communities on Reddit cross-post high-signal tweets within 90 seconds. 
- **The Secret Sauce:** The Reddit comment section is **Shaz's authentic comedic voice**. Community upvotes push the funniest roasts, counter-arguments, and reality checks straight to the top.

## 3. How to Turn a Scoop into a Shaz Script

When the scout extracts a breaking post and its top comments:

### Step 1: Identify the Tension
* **The News (Fact):** What just got leaked or announced?
* **The Audience Reaction (Roast):** Why is the audience rolling their eyes or freaking out?

### Step 2: Formulate the Shaz Angle (The 5 Templates)
* If fans are exhausted by hype $\rightarrow$ **The Death of a Concept** (*"The quiet death of..."*)
* If the rumor sounds too good to be true $\rightarrow$ **The System Decoder** (*"Why this leak is actually a marketing test"*)
* If one specific detail matters more than the rest $\rightarrow$ **The Hyper-Specific Absolute** (*"The only spec that actually matters"*)

### Step 3: Extract the Hook from the Top Comment
* Never start like an anchor (*"Today Jason Schreier reported..."*).
* Start with the audience sentiment (*"We waited ten years for GTA 6, but everyone is ignoring the one detail that actually ruins it."*).
