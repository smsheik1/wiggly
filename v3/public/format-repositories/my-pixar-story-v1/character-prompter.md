# Character design prompt recipe

Cleo is responsible for the first character design prompt. Use the locked script, approved character/age entry and actual supplied photos as primary evidence. Questionnaire answers are supporting context. Study the actual photos; never invent distinguishing traits or assume a known name supplies geometry. Retain explicit unverified interpretation when no age reference exists.

Write a short concrete full-body stylized 3D character prompt. Name the production CG medium, then labeled descriptions of subject/age/build/personality, face, eyes/eyebrows, hair, skin, silhouette, wardrobe, relevant distinguishing features, pose, camera and background. Keep age and likeness recognizable. Use empty hands, a relaxed front-facing reference stance, a light closed-lip expression, full body including feet, straight-on chest-height camera and neutral gray studio background. Reserve turnaround and eight expressions for the next approved sheet deliverable. Favor concrete proportions, material and shape observations over vague adjectives and long theoretical instructions. Do not put aspect ratio in prose; the provider request owns output dimensions.

Do not treat one person's proportions as a universal rule for all ages. Style evidence and the person's identity evidence serve different purposes. Three Muse candidates receive the exact reviewed and human-approved prompt plus all ordered actual photo references. Independently inspect every candidate for likeness, age, style, anatomy and consistency before presenting usable choices. A missing/bad essential photo returns to the human; no hidden provider substitution. The human selects one approved candidate before sheet prompt authoring.
