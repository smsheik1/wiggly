# My Pixar Story — 3D Animated Family Memoir

Transform a family's questionnaire answers into an emotional 72-second 3D animated film.

## Trigger phrases
- "Make a Pixar-style story for my daughter"
- "3D animated family memoir from our memories"
- "Warm big-eyed feature-animation film about my dad"
- "Pixar-style tribute for my mom's birthday"

## Deep Agent Pipeline
1. **Story Director (Story First, Beats & Thesis):** Reads questionnaire memories, plans emotional approaches, extracts the closing thesis, and locks 5-7 dramatic beats. Mandatory approval gate before spending GPU credits.
2. **Character & Prop Architect (4K Continuity Lineup):** Synthesizes full-body 3D Pixar character lineups and hero prop sheets (vehicles, instruments, keepsakes) with Nano Banana 2.1 to guarantee character and object continuity across every shot.
3. **Location Scout (3D Environment Plates):** Designs spatial 16:9 environment master plates with warm volumetric lighting, storybook proportions, and camera depth of field.
4. **Storyboard Artist (8-Panel Directorial Sheets):** Draws native 16:9 storyboard panels locking camera moves, character eye lines, and physical staging for human director approval.
5. **Seedance Compiler & Native Audio Engine:** Compiles declarative scene prompts into Seedance 2.5 with frame-locked lip movement, acoustic ambience, and native character voices in a single multimodal pass.
6. **The Producer & Lossless Stitching:** Losslessly stitches finished 1080p clips into a continuous master movie with automated dailies contact sheets.
